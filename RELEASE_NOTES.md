## UniLink Android v1.7.0

### Smoother interface
A motion pass over the whole app: a sliding navigation bar, direction-aware tab transitions, a morphing status badge, spring press feedback with light haptics, staggered card entry, animated lists, and predictive Back.

### Also included (1.6.1)
Battery fix: the CPU and Wi-Fi locks are held only while UniLink is connecting, signed in, or retrying on campus Wi-Fi.

### Update
Install `UniLink-v1.7.0.apk` over the previous version; accounts and settings are kept. The signing certificate SHA-256 is at the bottom of these notes (see SECURITY.md).
