## UniLink Android v1.10.6

- **Data bar on the Connection tab** for the account in use, with a Refresh button; refreshes itself once when you open the tab if the numbers are older than 30 minutes (nothing runs in the background).
- The bar shows the data **left** (green, amber below 30%, red below 10%).
- Wording: "just now" instead of "0 minutes ago".
- Data usage needs campus Wi-Fi without a VPN tunnel for `gate1.urmia.ac.ir`.
