## UniLink Android v1.10.0

- **Remaining data per account.** The Accounts tab now shows a usage bar (remaining / allowance, renewal date) for every account, read from the university user portal with the account's own user name and password. Tap **Refresh data usage** to update; nothing runs in the background.
- The user-portal certificate is pinned: approve its SHA-256 fingerprint once and nothing is sent before that. The app signs out of the user portal after each reading.
- Includes the 1.9.2 timing logs (Activity > Share log) for tracing slow connections.

### Update
Install `UniLink-v1.10.0.apk` over the previous version; accounts and settings are kept. The signing certificate SHA-256 is at the bottom of these notes (see SECURITY.md).

> Unofficial project, not affiliated with any university or with Sophos. Use only accounts you are allowed to use.
