## UniLink Android v1.2.0

### What's new
- **Persian interface (فارسی)** with proper right-to-left layout, covering every screen, dialog, status message, the notification and the Quick Settings tile. By default it follows your phone's language. Switch any time in **Settings → Language** (System / English / فارسی).
- The Activity log stays in English so it is easy to share when reporting a problem.

### Also in 1.1.x
- First-run checklist, Quick Settings tile, detection of an already-live portal session, and a fix for the notification status.

### Install
Download `UniLink-v1.2.0.apk` below and install it over the previous version. Your accounts and settings are kept.

Verify the download with `UniLink-v1.2.0.apk.sha256` if you like. This is a debug-signed build.
