## UniLink Android v1.8.1

- The Activity log card no longer runs under the tab bar.
- The tab bar is opaque, so scrolled text no longer shows through it (Light, Dark and Midnight).
- Content fades softly at the top edge instead of being cut off.

### Update
Install `UniLink-v1.8.1.apk` over the previous version; accounts and settings are kept. The signing certificate SHA-256 is at the bottom of these notes (see SECURITY.md).
