## UniLink Android v1.4.0

### What's new
- **New glass-style interface.** Frosted cards over a soft gradient, a floating navigation bar, and a big animated status badge on the Connection tab. It follows your phone's light or dark theme.
- **Home-screen widget.** Long-press the home screen, add **UniLink**, and you get the status plus a Connect / Pause button.
- **Free software.** UniLink is now licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later, and ships the metadata needed for F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid.

### Install
Download `UniLink-v1.4.0.apk` below and install it over the previous version. Your accounts and settings are kept.

Verify the download with `UniLink-v1.4.0.apk.sha256` if you like. Signed with the project's debug key.
